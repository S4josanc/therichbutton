Your purchase. Your crown.

These rules apply to The Richest Button, a digital novelty product sold by Akadémia eñe s.r.o.

What you buy

Your purchase includes a personal virtual button and a digital keepsake. You also take the public title of The Richest Button when your verified payment meets the current minimum. It is a playful digital title, with no financial return, investment value or physical product.

Amount and payment

Choose any amount at least €1 above the current owner’s amount. The amount shown in euros is the total for this purchase, including any applicable taxes. Stripe shows the final total before you confirm. This is a one-time payment, not a subscription. All new purchases use Stripe.

The title can change hands

Your title lasts until a new qualifying higher payment is confirmed. If another owner takes the crown later, you keep your personal button and keepsake. Being overtaken does not automatically refund the earlier purchase.

The minimum may change while you are paying. If your payment no longer meets the minimum when it is processed for the title, it will not replace the owner and the payment is refunded automatically. Your order page shows the outcome. Your bank may take additional time to show a refund.

Your name and your keepsake

Your display name or alias and country are optional and public if supplied. Your payment amount is always public. After a successful purchase, keep the private order link to return to your button. You can share it by WhatsApp, email, or another method. Anyone with the link can view the keepsake; sharing it does not transfer ownership.

Support and your rights

For a payment, access or refund problem, contact support@therichbutton.com. If payment confirmation is delayed, check the order before paying again.

Nothing in these rules removes statutory consumer rights. The seller details, withdrawal information and remedies described in sections 1, 5, 6 and 8 of our general purchase terms also apply. Personal data is handled as described in our privacy notice.

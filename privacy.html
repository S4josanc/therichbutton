4. Providers

We use GitHub Pages to host the website, Supabase for application and database services, Stripe to process payments, Resend for transactional email, IONOS for support email, and Google Fonts to display typefaces. Providers may act as processors or independent controllers according to their role.

5. How long we keep data

We keep information for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including delivering and supporting gifts, securing the service and resolving disputes, and for longer where required by law. Slovak accounting records are generally retained for ten years following the year to which they relate. Retention of a gift link depends on providing the service; a recipient or buyer can contact us about deletion or access. Information is kept subject to legal retention requirements and rights of defence.

Where a transfer is subject to GDPR transfer rules, we rely on an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for more information about relevant safeguards.
